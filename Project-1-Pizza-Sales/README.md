# Pizza Sales Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of a full year of pizza sales transactions to identify revenue drivers, top-performing products, and sales patterns by day and category.

## Problem Statement
A pizza business needed visibility into:
- Which products and categories drive the most revenue
- Sales patterns by day of week
- Order value and volume trends across the year

## Data Source
- **Dataset:** Pizza Place Sales
- **Records:** 48,620 orders, full year 2015
- **Columns:** pizza_id, order_id, pizza_name_id, quantity, order_date, order_time, unit_price, total_price, pizza_size, pizza_category, pizza_ingredients, pizza_name
