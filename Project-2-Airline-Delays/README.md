# Airline Flight Delays Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of U.S. domestic flight delays and cancellations to identify patterns by airline, route, and day, and to understand the main drivers of disruption.

## Problem Statement
Airlines and airports need visibility into:
- Which carriers have the best/worst on-time performance
- What causes cancellations (weather, carrier, security, air system)
- Whether certain routes or airports are more delay-prone than others

## Data Source
- **Dataset:** 2015 Flight Delays and Cancellations (U.S. Department of Transportation, Bureau of Transportation Statistics)
- **Original size:** ~5.8 million flights, full year 2015
- **Portfolio slice used:** January 2015 only (170,475 flights) — trimmed from the full-year file to keep the interactive dashboard fast and responsive. The same measures and approach scale directly to the full year.
- **Columns:** flight date, airline, origin/destination airport, scheduled vs. actual departure/arrival, delay minutes by cause, cancellation reason, distance
